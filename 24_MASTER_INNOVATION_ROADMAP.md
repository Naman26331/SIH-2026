# 24 — Master Innovation Roadmap

## Vision
FLEET-X is an edge-native warehouse intelligence platform where the warehouse optimizes inventory and tasks while robots coordinate movement locally.

## Phase 1 — Core Fleet
ROS 2, Gazebo, 3+ robots, local navigation, decentralized state/intent sharing, conflict detection, reservations, and dynamic rerouting.

## Phase 2 — Resilience
Deadlock detection, starvation prevention, priority management, robot failure recovery, network-loss mode, and battery-aware dispatch.

## Phase 3 — Warehouse Intelligence
Task auction, demand prediction, congestion prediction, inventory placement, order batching, and inventory rebalancing.

## Phase 4 — Digital Twin
Live map, fleet state, event history, heatmaps, replay, and what-if simulation.

## Phase 5 — Safety & Trust
Human-aware routing, authenticated communication, cybersecurity, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance.

## Phase 6 — Benchmarking
Automatic scenario generation, baseline comparison, repeated seeds, KPI collection, and statistical reporting.

## Phase 7 — Future
Learning-based optimization, multi-warehouse coordination, advanced human prediction, and warehouse-layout optimization.

## Priority
### MUST HAVE
3+ robots, ROS 2/Gazebo, communication, intent sharing, conflict management, reservations, rerouting, task allocation, dashboard, benchmarking.

### HIGH VALUE
Deadlock recovery, battery, failures, network resilience, congestion prediction, inventory intelligence.

### INNOVATION
Human collaboration, cybersecurity, predictive maintenance, what-if simulation, and multi-warehouse expansion.

## Credibility Rule
Clearly label every feature as implemented, simulated, prototype, or future roadmap. Do not present unimplemented features as production-ready.
